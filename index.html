What documents do I need to register a trade licence?

A passport copy, a copy of your UAE residence or tourist visa and Emirates ID, and — if you're a resident — a No Objection Certificate from your current sponsor where required.

What is a General Trading Licence, and what does it cover?

It allows trading in the goods listed in the activity classification guide, except activities that need specialised external approval — for example pharmaceuticals or food.

What are the steps to issue a General Trading Licence?

1) Get preliminary approval. 2) Reserve your trade name. 3) Obtain any external approvals. 4) Sign legal documents such as the MOA. 5) Secure registered premises and complete licensing.

What is a Mainland Licence?

A licence issued by the emirate's Department of Economy that lets you operate anywhere in the UAE and internationally, usually with the widest trading flexibility.

What are Free Zones?

Designated economic areas offering benefits such as 100% foreign ownership, tax advantages and fast setup. Trading directly in the mainland may require extra arrangements.

Why can't a Free Zone company trade directly in the Mainland?

Free zone companies are regulated by their free zone authority rather than the mainland DED. To sell onshore you may need a local distributor, a branch or a mainland licence.

What are the rules for reserving a trade name?

No offensive or restricted words, no country or government names, no punctuation, and a person's name only if they are a partner or owner. The activity should be reflected where required.

Which licence covers both commercial and professional activities?

Generally a Commercial Licence.

What are external approvals?

Permissions from authorities that regulate specific sectors — health, safety, environment, education or specialised commerce — required before certain activities can be licensed.

How do I open a branch of a GCC company?

The board resolution must be executed in the home Gulf country, attested by the UAE Embassy, translated into Arabic and endorsed by the relevant UAE authorities.
