# 2Business-Merged

**UAE Business Setup & PRO Services Website**

A minimalist, responsive landing page for a Dubai business setup and PRO services company. It merges the content of two existing service sites (3S Business Services and PRO Deskk) under one placeholder brand, **3S ProDesk**.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no build step: open `index.html` in a browser.

## Sections

- Hero with a stylised Dubai skyline and key stats
- Jurisdiction strip and 10 jurisdiction cards (DWTC, Mainland, Meydan, IFZA, RAKEZ, Shams, SPC, DMCC, DIFC, ADGM)
- Four starting points: Mainland, Free Zone, Employment Visa, Golden Visa
- Tabbed services: Company Formation, Visas & PRO, Corporate, Documents
- About / corporate PRO services
- Business setup packages with prices, plus the VIP Fast Track add-on
- Monthly PRO plans (Silver, Gold, Diamond) with a team-size toggle
- Six-step setup process
- Interactive cost calculator that sends the quote request to WhatsApp
- Company-structure offers, testimonials, team, FAQ accordion
- Contact form (sends to WhatsApp), two offices, footer with newsletter
